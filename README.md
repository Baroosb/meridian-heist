MERIDIAN HEIST — One Last Job at the Meridian Bank. A top-down stealth heist game: cross the Meridian Bank unseen, dodge guard vision cones and CCTV, beat the laser grid, grab the Aurora Diamond and escape to the van before the alarm maxes.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 (or run `npx serve game`).

## Play

Vex, a retired thief, is pulled back for one last job: the Aurora Diamond in the Meridian Bank vault. Cross three floors — Lobby & Tellers, Gallery & Offices, The Vault — unseen. Guard vision cones (amber, red when they spot you) fill the ALARM meter fast; CCTV cones fill it slower. Slip the laser grid or kill it at the wall switch, grab the diamond and the cash, and reach the getaway van before the alarm hits 100 or the timer runs out. Stay unseen for the GHOST bonus.

- **WASD / arrows** — move (click to move also works)
- **Shift** — sneak (harder to spot, slower)
- **E** — grab loot / use the laser switch
- **Q** — smoke pellet (blocks cones, 3 per floor)
- **P** — pause · **M** — mute
